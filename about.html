About the project

A practical agriculture information portal

AgroGuide Pro brings the project's crop dataset and planning features into a clearer, connected website experience.

Project purpose

Why AgroGuide Pro exists

The project is designed to make its crop information easier to explore and use. Crop profiles connect seasons, soil preferences, crop timelines, input estimates and practical notes in one place.

Its goal is to support learning and basic planning by helping users compare the data available in the project, rather than replacing local expertise.

What it includes

Eight connected pages

The portal includes a home page, ten crop profiles, fertilizer and manpower estimates, an input cost estimator, soil recommendations, a season planner, crop comparison and data-backed farming notes.

All crop values are loaded from the existing local data/crops.json file.

Project team

Student project

AgroGuide Pro was built by Priyansu Kumar Soni, Harsh Kumar and Arun Kushwaha as a BCA final-year minor project.

Use with care

Demonstration information

Crop recommendations and estimates reflect the project's demonstration dataset. Local climate, soil tests, varieties and farming practices differ; confirm important decisions with a qualified local agricultural adviser.
